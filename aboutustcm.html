    What is the TCM?

    The Trinity College Meeting (TCM) is the student government of Trinity College, Toronto. The TCM is unique among student governments at UofT because it is a direct democracy: every fee-paying Trinity student is a voting member of the TCM. The TCM also has a number of elected officials that make up the TCM Executive, and several subcommitees that manage certain aspects of student governance. However, at the end of the day, it is the general TCM that has the final say.

    The TCM manages and funds Trinity's student clubs. The Board of Stewards (TCBS) approves club mandates, and the Finance Committee (FC) approves club budgets. The TCM also funds and supports Trinity's two big dances: The Saint's Ball (aka Saints) and Conversatizone (aka Conversat).

    The TCM supports student life, by supporting College events like the high tables, and by running our own events through the efforts of the Chair, the Heads of College, and for residence students, the Heads of Arts, and for non-resident students, the Heads of NRAC. The aforementioned heads are also available to support students individually through office hours or personal meetings. The Equity Co-Chairs and the Equity committee support equity and sustainability within the student body.

    Finally, the TCM, especially the executive team, communicates student concerns to the College administration. It is through the work of TCM advocacy that high tables and gowns were brought back, a student levy avoided, and a promised moratorium on further fee raises secured.
